# Recruitment screener - unissued, no responses collected

Target: 12-15 actual adult participants, with an app customization event in the last six months. Aim for different starting skills and at least some users of two or more themeable apps. Recruitment channels must be reviewed for community rules before posting, and no personal data should be collected without consent.

## Neutral intake questions

1. Which apps have you changed colors, themes or appearance settings in during the last six months? List the apps and approximate dates, if you recall.
2. How did you make your most recent change? (Downloaded a theme / adjusted built-in settings / edited configuration or code / other / can't remember.)
3. About how much time did that last change take, including any troubleshooting? (Under 5m / 5-15m / 15-45m / over 45m / unknown.)
4. Did you customize more than one application in that time? (Yes / no / unsure.)
5. Would you be willing to demonstrate the steps of a past customization and try a prototype in a 25-30 minute moderated session? (Yes / no.)

## Research record requirements

Use anonymized participant IDs only after an actual participant responds. For each participant capture consent, qualifying app history, observed past behavior, task outcomes, contradictory evidence and allowed notes. Do not prefill invented answers or treat interest as proof of demand.

Discovery and usability sessions can overlap with G1 prototyping; the go/no-go decision for broad investment must be based on observed evidence, not the existence of the interview plan.
